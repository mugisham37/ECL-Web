Security & trust

How we protect your loan data.

Written plainly. We'd rather under-claim and be verifiable than over-claim and erode the trust this product depends on.

Encryption

All traffic is served over HTTPS only. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest. Uploaded workbooks and generated outputs are stored in encrypted object storage; database volumes are encrypted at the disk level.

Data residency

Hosted tenants are provisioned in a single region of your choosing. For institutions with strict residency requirements, the platform can be deployed on-premise or in your private cloud — the same container image runs in both.

Authentication

Username and password with modern password hashing (Argon2/bcrypt class). Sessions expire after a configurable idle period. Login attempts are rate-limited and logged. SAML/OIDC single sign-on is available for Enterprise tenants.

Audit trail

Every state-changing action — login, upload, run start, role change, soft-delete — is recorded with the user, timestamp and tenant. Each run stores the SHA-256 hash of its inputs and outputs alongside the engine version used.

Contact us for our latest penetration-test summary and SOC 2 status.

Backups & recovery

Tenant data is backed up daily with a recovery objective of 24 hours or better. Runs are soft-deleted only; inputs and outputs are retained for a configurable window — seven years by default — to match financial-records norms.

Compliance

The platform is built against OWASP Top-10 hardening guidance, with tenant isolation enforced at the data-access layer and verified by automated tests. Formal certifications are listed in your contract addendum as they are completed.

Incident response

Security events follow a documented response process with defined severities, containment steps and tenant notification commitments. Affected tenants are notified directly; we do not publish other tenants' incidents.

Sub-processors

We maintain a current list of sub-processors (hosting, object storage, email delivery for authentication). The list and our data-processing agreement are available to prospective and active tenants on request.
